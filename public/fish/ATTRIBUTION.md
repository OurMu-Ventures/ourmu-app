# Fish sprite attribution

The login-page aquarium (`src/components/FishCanvas.tsx`) renders these fish
from photographs sourced on Wikimedia Commons. Each was background-removed,
cropped, mildly colour-graded, and re-encoded to a 384px-wide WebP. All three
are Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike; attribution is retained here.

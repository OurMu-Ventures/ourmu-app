# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment.
Be constructive, assume good intent, respect privacy, and focus criticism on
the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, publication of private
data, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit,
remove, reject, or lock contributions and interactions that violate these
expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to `community@ourmu.org`. Do not include
unnecessary personal or confidential information in a report.
